swarmsim replays

Recorded flights from the swarmsim simulator. Eight quadcopters fly from their starts to their goals while a defence platform fires at anything inside its range. Every model flies the same starts and goals in each episode, so you can switch models and compare. Nothing is simulated in your browser: these are replays of runs made with PyBullet, recorded ten times a second.
